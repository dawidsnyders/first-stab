# Kamino Fixed Rates Launch Strategy Analysis

## Research Summary

Based on analysis of Kamino's history and leading tech/fintech product launches (Linear, Stripe, Figma, Coinbase), this document captures key insights to inform the Fixed Rates launch campaign.

---

## 1. Kamino's Historical Context

### Previous Major Launches

**KMNO Token Airdrop (March-April 2024)**
- Announced airdrop date in March, snapshot March 31, token launch April
- Generated significant buzz but also controversy
- Required mid-campaign adjustments ("We hear you" post after blowback)
- **Lesson:** Transparency and community sentiment management are critical

**Kamino Lend V2 (Q4 2024)**
- Positioned around modularity and permissionless market creation
- Framed as evolution of battle-tested infrastructure
- Emphasized security credentials (audits, formal verification)
- **Lesson:** Lead with track record and security for institutional audiences

**Breakpoint 2024 Announcement**
- Showcased growth: $10M TVL at previous Breakpoint → $2B market size
- Second-highest fee-producing lending protocol in DeFi
- Positioned Kamino as infrastructure for all user types
- **Lesson:** Growth metrics and vision framing resonate

### @y2kappa (Marius) - Co-Founder Profile
- Bloomberg background, technical credibility
- "Solana bear market survivor" positioning
- Active on thought leadership (open source advocacy, risk explanations)
- Posts "hot takes" and technical explainers
- **Style:** Technical authority + ecosystem conviction

## 2. Industry Launch Playbooks

### Linear - "Build in Public" + Founder-Led Growth

**What Made It Work:**
- Founders had large Twitter followings before launch
- Built waitlist to 10,000+ through "building in public"
- Announced company before product was built
- Sequoia approached them 2 days after Twitter announcement
- $35K lifetime marketing spend → $400M valuation

**Key Tactics:**
- Founder narrative control ("hot takes" aligned with brand)
- Multiple launch moments (seed raise, beta, pricing, features)
- Early beta users from future unicorns (Cohere, Runway, Ramp)
- Cult-like following through quality + consistency

**Applicable to Kamino:**
- Marius's thread is critical - founder credibility matters
- Multiple launch moments (announcement, live, integrations)
- Target sophisticated early users who amplify

---

### Figma - "Rolling Thunder" Launches

**What Made It Work:**
- "Rolling thunder" = collection of features released at different times under one umbrella message
- Recognized that most customers miss announcements
- Customer-facing teams as best "marketing channel"
- Personalized outreach from sales teams with "hit lists"

**Key Tactics:**
- Avoid launch fatigue from too many big releases
- Hammer message home over multiple releases
- Arm support teams with saved replies
- Annual conference (Config) as anchor event

**Applicable to Kamino:**
- Post-launch should be "rolling thunder" of integrations
- BD team (Cheryl/Mark) with deck = "hit list" approach
- In-app explainers = customer-facing team enablement

---

### Stripe - Conference-Anchored + Developer Community

**What Made It Work:**
- Annual Sessions conference as primary launch vehicle
- 50+ features announced at once (big bang)
- Social media used to amplify, engage developers
- Consistent messaging: "Grow the GDP of the internet"

**Key Tactics:**
- Mission-driven framing
- Demonstrate scale ($1T+ processed)
- Focus on solving customer complexity
- Developer community engagement

**Applicable to Kamino:**
- Fixed Rates already announced at Breakpoint (conference anchor done)
- Mission framing: "Bridge between DeFi and tokenized assets"
- Track record ($100B+ loans, $0 bad debt) = Stripe-like credibility

---

### Coinbase - Consistent + Narrative Control

**What Made It Work:**
- Never stopped advertising through crypto cycles
- "Update the System" campaign questioning TradFi
- Stunt campaigns during bear market ("Long Live Crypto")
- Retention + diversification strategy

**Key Tactics:**
- Counter-narrative positioning (DeFi > TradFi)
- Product showcases as events
- "Everything Exchange" positioning
- AI + innovation forward

**Applicable to Kamino:**
- "Institutions need this" = counter-narrative to variable rates
- FalconX as proof point of institutional adoption
- "Infrastructure layer" positioning

## 3. DeFi-Specific Launch Patterns

### What Works in Crypto Twitter

1. **KOL Amplification**
   - GMX, Pendle used long-term partnerships with respected analysts
   - Double-digit TVL growth from influencer relationships
   - Micro-influencers for niche targeting (DeFi specialists)

2. **Community-Driven FOMO**
   - Gamified engagement (badges, tasks)
   - Airdrops and early access programs
   - Meme-ability and cultural resonance

3. **Multi-Channel Hybrid**
   - Twitter as primary, Discord/Telegram for depth
   - YouTube for education
   - Press for legitimacy signal

4. **Reward Early Adopters**
   - Uniswap $UNI airdrop = masterclass in loyalty
   - "OG user" recognition drives advocacy

---

## 4. Key Insights for Fixed Rates Launch

### What Sets This Launch Apart

1. **Institutional-First Positioning**
   - Unlike typical DeFi launches (retail-first)
   - Requires credibility signals: track record, security, partnerships
   - FalconX quote is premium social proof

2. **Complex Product, Simple Value Prop**
   - "Fixed rate. Fixed term." = crystal clear
   - Technical depth exists (FR PRIVATE) but don't lead with it
   - Education required but shouldn't overwhelm

3. **Pre-Announced (Breakpoint)**
   - Audience knows it's coming
   - Campaign = delivery on promise
   - Risk: Anti-climactic if not executed well

4. **Infrastructure Play, Not Feature**
   - This is foundational to Kamino's institutional pivot
   - Positions future products (RWA markets, Multiply integration)
   - Higher stakes than feature launch

## 5. Recommended Launch Framework

### Phase 1: Build-Up (1-2 weeks before launch)

**Objective:** Reinforce awareness, build anticipation, educate

**Content Types:**
- Educational threads explaining why fixed rates matter
- "The problem with variable rates" content
- Institutional use case teasers
- Behind-the-scenes development posts
- Countdown content

**Key Messages:**
- DeFi's rate volatility problem is solved
- Institutions can finally participate
- Battle-tested infrastructure, new capability

---

### Phase 2: Launch Day (Big Bang)

**Objective:** Maximum coordinated impact

**Timing:** All assets go live within 2-hour window

**Sequence:**
1. **T-0:** @kamino main announcement thread
2. **T+15min:** @y2kappa co-founder thread
3. **T+30min:** Blog post live
4. **T+1hr:** KOL posts begin (staggered across 4 KOLs)
5. **T+2hr:** Telegram announcement
6. **T+same day:** Press coverage goes live
7. **All day:** Team personal accounts RT/QT

**Amplification Tactics:**
- Pin announcement thread
- Reply to own thread with key points
- QT partner/KOL posts
- Engage with every meaningful reply

---

### Phase 3: Post-Launch (Rolling Thunder)

**Week 1: Education + Traction**
- How-to content
- First borrower highlights
- Stats posts (volume, rates locked)

**Week 2-4: Expand Narrative**
- Use case deep dives
- Institutional testimonials
- Integration announcements (new markets)

**Month 2+: Sustain**
- Multiply integration announcement
- RWA market launches
- Case studies

## 7. Tone & Voice Guidelines

Based on Kamino's existing presence:

**DO:**
- Lead with technical credibility
- Use data and specific metrics
- Be direct and confident
- Acknowledge DeFi-native audience intelligence
- Show, don't just tell (examples, scenarios)

**DON'T:**
- Overhype or use superlatives without backing
- Be condescending to retail users
- Ignore community concerns (KMNO lesson)
- Sacrifice clarity for technical jargon

**Voice Characteristics:**
- Authoritative but not arrogant
- Technical but accessible
- Ambitious but grounded in track record
- Institutional-grade professionalism

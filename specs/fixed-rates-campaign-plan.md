## Content Deliverables

### 1. Main Announcement Thread (@kamino)

**Format:** 8-10 tweet thread
**Owner:** Colyn (draft), You (review)

**Structure:**
```
1/ Introducing Kamino Fixed Rates: Fixed-rate, fixed-term borrowing infrastructure on Solana.

Launching today with @FalconX as our first institutional fixed-rate borrower.

[Announcement graphic]

2/ The problem: Almost all DeFi credit runs on variable rates. Rates can swing from 3% to 10% overnight.

For institutions and RWA strategies, this is untenable.

3/ The solution: With Fixed Rates, borrowers lock in their cost of capital for a defined term.

The rate you see is the rate you pay. Day one through expiry.

4/ How it works:
• Select your rate and duration
• Execute in a single transaction
• Your rate is locked for the entire term
• Auto-rollover available at maturity

[Product screenshot/graphic]

5/ Why this matters:

Institutions need predictable funding costs.
RWA strategies need fixed cost of capital.
Carry trades need rate certainty.

Fixed Rates delivers all three.

6/ "Kamino Lend has evolved into a critical credit instrument for our desk... By locking in fixed rates, we can minimize rate volatility and duration-match in a unique way within the DeFi ecosystem."

– Craig Birchall, Head of Lending, @FalconX

7/ Built on battle-tested infrastructure:
• 2+ years live
• $100B+ loans originated
• $0 bad debt
• 18 audits, 3 formal verifications
• Open source & verifiably built

8/ What's next:
• Borrow Intents (coming soon)
• Fixed rates in new markets
• RWA integrations
• Kamino Multiply support

This is just the beginning.

9/ Get started:

[Link to product]
[Link to blog post]
[Link to docs]

For institutions: institutions@kamino.finance

10/ Fixed rate. Fixed term. Fixed Rates.

The foundation for institutional credit onchain is here.
```

### 2. Co-Founder Thread (@y2kappa)

**Format:** 6-8 tweet thread
**Owner:** Marius

**Tone:** Technical authority, personal perspective, vision

**Structure:**
```
1/ Today we're launching Fixed Rates.

This is the single most important piece of credit infrastructure for institutions and RWAs in DeFi.

Let me explain why we built it and what it means.

2/ I spent years in traditional finance. One thing was always true: serious players need predictable cost of capital.

Variable rates work for retail. They don't work for institutions managing quarterly P&Ls.

3/ The problem with utilization-based rates:

When someone borrows $30M from a $100M pool, everyone's rate doubles overnight.

Neither borrowers nor lenders control what they'll pay or receive tomorrow.

[Optional: graphic from FR PRIVATE]

4/ Fixed Rates changes this fundamentally.

Borrowers lock their rate for 1, 3, or 6 months. No surprises. No rate spikes at 2am. Just predictable cost of capital.

5/ What excites me most: this creates DeFi's first real yield curve.

Different rates for different durations. Term structure. Price discovery driven by supply and demand.

This is how real credit markets work.

6/ We built this on the same infrastructure that's processed $100B+ in loans with zero bad debt.

Same security. Same reliability. New capability.

7/ This is just the first step.

Borrow Intents is next - letting borrowers become makers, not takers.

Then RWA markets. Then Multiply integration.

The infrastructure for institutional DeFi is being built. On Kamino. On Solana.

8/ Try it: [link]
Read more: [blog link]

If you're an institution looking to borrow: institutions@kamino.finance
```

---

## Product Overview

### What Fixed Rates Solves
- **The Problem:** Almost all DeFi credit markets operate on floating rates that can swing from 3% to 10% overnight. This is untenable for institutions and RWA strategies.
- **The Solution:** Fixed-rate, fixed-term borrowing where the rate you see is the rate you pay for the entire duration.

### Key Innovations
1. **Fixed Rate + Fixed Term:** Lock in borrowing cost for defined periods (1m, 3m, 6m, etc.)
2. **Conditional Liquidity:** Lenders signal availability with zero opportunity cost
3. **Borrow Intents:** Borrowers become makers, not takers - real price discovery
4. **Auto-Rollover:** Loans refinance automatically if liquidity permits
5. **DeFi's First Yield Curve:** Term structure emerges from the rate-duration grid

### Foundation
- Same battle-tested Kamino Lend infrastructure (2+ years live)
- $100B+ loans originated
- $0 bad debt
- 18 audits, 3 formal verifications
- Open source, verifiably built

### Post-Launch Roadmap
- Fixed rates into new markets beyond Main Market
- RWA looping use-cases enabled
- Kamino Multiply integration ("where the big bucks comes in")

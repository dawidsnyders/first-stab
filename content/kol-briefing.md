## Product Summary

### What is Kamino Fixed Rates?

Kamino Fixed Rates is fixed-rate, fixed-term borrowing infrastructure on Solana. Borrowers can lock in their interest rate for a defined period (1 month, 3 months, etc.), providing predictable cost of capital regardless of market conditions.

### The Problem It Solves

Almost all DeFi lending uses variable rates based on pool utilization. These rates can swing from 3% to 10%+ overnight. This makes it impossible for:
- Institutions to budget financing costs
- RWA strategies to maintain predictable spreads
- Carry traders to sustain positions when rates spike
- Brokers to offer fixed rates to clients

### Why It Matters

Fixed Rates is the foundation for institutional adoption of DeFi credit. It enables use cases that were previously impossible on-chain.

## FAQ

**Q: Can anyone use Fixed Rates or is it just for institutions?**
A: Anyone can use it. The product is permissionless. We're highlighting institutions because this unlocks use cases they specifically need.

**Q: What rates are available?**
A: Rates and terms depend on market conditions and available liquidity. Users can see available rates in the product UI.

**Q: What happens at loan maturity?**
A: Borrowers can repay, roll over to a new fixed term (if liquidity available), or fall back to variable rates.

**Q: Is this a new smart contract?**
A: Fixed Rates runs on the same battle-tested Kamino Lend contracts that have been live for 2+ years.

**Q: What about Borrow Intents?**
A: Borrow Intents is coming next. It lets borrowers post their desired loan terms on-chain for lenders to fill—like a limit order for credit.
